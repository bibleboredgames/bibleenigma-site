How to play

Two kinds of puzzle, both built on a verse of the King James Bible. Here is everything you need, in plain words.

  1. Verse Cipher
  2. Missing Words
  3. Clues
  4. Ads
  5. The daily verse

Verse Cipher

The main game. A verse is written in numbers, and you work out which letter each number stands for.

Numbers stand for letters

Every letter of the verse is replaced by a number. The same number is always the same letter, everywhere in the verse. Choose a square, then type the letter you think it hides.

Some letters are given

Each puzzle starts with a few letters already in place. Use them, and what you know of Scripture, to deduce the rest, one square at a time, until the verse is whole.

Three mistakes

Every wrong letter counts as a mistake, and you have three. At the third, you can choose to watch a short ad to take one back and keep going, or see your results. A puzzle you don’t finish can be tried again at no cost.

Hidden words

On Advanced and Expert, some words have no numbers at all. Their lines are gold. Work them out from the words around them.

Frozen words and fiery squares

On Advanced and Expert, a word may be frozen under ice. Somewhere else in the verse are fiery squares, marked with a flame. Each fiery square you fill melts a layer of ice. When the ice is gone, the word thaws and you can solve it.

Five difficulties

From familiar verses with many letters given, to lesser-known verses with few.

Long words

Letters and numbers always stay full size. When a word is too wide for your screen, the board scrolls sideways, so slide it to see the whole word.

Missing Words

A second kind of puzzle, for well-known verses.

Fill the blanks

A few words are taken out of the verse. Choose the right word for each blank from the word bank beneath it. A wrong word counts as a mistake, and you have three, as in Verse Cipher.

A Missing Words verse of the day

Alongside the daily Verse Cipher, there is a Missing Words verse of the day, the same for every player.

Clues

You never need a clue to finish a puzzle, but they help when you are stuck.

Star squares

Some squares carry a small star. Type the right letter there and you collect the star. Every three stars earn one clue, and leftover stars carry over to your next puzzle.

Two kinds of clue

Buy a letter
Choose a letter, and every square where it appears is filled in. If it isn’t in the verse, you are told so.
Reveal a letter
Choose a square, and only that square is filled in.

You can hold up to two of each. In Missing Words, the clue is Reveal a word. A puzzle finished without clues or mistakes earns a bonus.

Ads, only if you choose

There are no banner ads and no ads between puzzles.

Rewarded ads are optional

When you are out of clues, or at your third mistake, you can choose to watch a short ad for a clue or to keep going. No puzzle requires one. If an ad can’t be shown, you still get the reward. You can watch up to ten a day.

The daily verse

Every day there is one daily puzzle, the same verse for every player on the same date.

Your streak

Your streak counts the days in a row you solve a puzzle. An optional reminder tells you when the day’s verse is ready.

Sharing without spoilers

After the daily, you can share your result: the date, whether you solved it, and your clues, mistakes and time. The verse itself is never in it, so friends can still solve it fresh.

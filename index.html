How to play

One verse a day, the same for everyone

Every day there is one daily verse, and every player gets the same one on the same date. Nothing about it is shown before you solve it, so the words are yours to find. When the last letter falls into place, you read the verse whole, with its reference and a short line of context.

It takes a few minutes. Done each morning, or each evening, it becomes a small and steady habit of reading Scripture.

  • StreakCounts the days in a row you solve a puzzle.
  • ReminderAn optional note when the day’s verse is ready. You choose whether to turn it on.
  • SharingShare your result as a short line of text that never gives the verse away.

Inside the game

Two ways to play

Verse Cipher

Every letter of the verse is replaced by a number, and the same number always means the same letter. A few letters are given. You deduce the rest, with three mistakes allowed, across five difficulties from Beginner to Expert.

How Verse Cipher works

Missing Words

A well-known verse with a few words taken out. Choose the right word for each blank from the word bank beneath it.

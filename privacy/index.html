Bible Enigma

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

Bible Enigma is a Bible word-puzzle game made by Bible Bored Games (Luke Andrew Hess). This policy explains what the app collects, why, who else handles it, and how to delete it.

The short version

  • You don't have to sign in. The first time the app goes online, it makes a guest account for you with a random account ID, and saves your game progress on our servers so it isn't lost.
  • If you also sign in (Apple, Google or email), we add your email address so your progress follows you to other devices. We use this data only to run the game.
  • The app shows optional ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some data to show and measure them. On iPhone, Google may use your device's ad ID for tracking only if you tap "Allow" on Apple's tracking prompt.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete account.

"Tracking" means linking data from this app with data from other companies' apps and websites for advertising. If you tap "Ask App Not to Track", Google does not get your advertising ID. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU and UK), the app asks for your consent first. The app never asks before you have finished your first puzzle.

Google handles this data under its own policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

What stays on your device

Your puzzles in progress, settings and daily reminders stay on your phone. Reminders are scheduled on the device; no server is involved. The app does not send usage analytics anywhere.

Deleting your data

  • Delete your account: open Settings → Delete account in the app, whether you are a guest or signed in. This permanently deletes your account, every sign-in linked to it (Apple, Google or email) and all your progress on our servers, all at once. It cannot be undone.
  • Erase this device: Settings → Erase data on this device clears progress stored on your phone. This is separate from deleting your account.
  • Stop Sign in with Apple or Google: you can also remove Bible Enigma from your Apple ID or Google account settings.
  • Ads: you can reset or limit your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

We keep your account data until you delete your account. You can also email us (below) to ask for a copy of your data, a correction or a deletion.

Children

Bible Enigma is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal data, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.

Bible Enigma

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

Bible Enigma is a Bible word-puzzle game made by Bible Bored Games (Luke Andrew Hess). This policy explains what the app collects, why we may use it, who else handles it, your rights, and how to delete it.

Who is responsible

Bible Bored Games (Luke Andrew Hess) is responsible for your data. In privacy law this is called the "controller". To reach us, email support@bibleenigma.app.

The short version

  • You don't have to sign in. The first time the app goes online, it makes a guest account for you with a random account ID, and saves your game progress on our servers so it isn't lost.
  • If you also sign in (Apple, Google or email), we add your email address so your progress follows you to other devices. We use this data only to run the game.
  • The app sends usage data (which puzzles are played and how they go) to PostHog, so we can see what to fix and improve. It is tied only to a random ID made on your phone, not to your name, email or account. In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland, the app sends usage data, and saves its random ID on your phone, only if you say yes on the consent form, including up to about the last 30 to 40 seconds of play before you answer. If you say no or don't answer, nothing is sent.
  • You can switch usage data off at any time in Settings → Your data.
  • The app shows optional ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some data to show and measure them. On iPhone, Google may use your device's ad ID for tracking only if you tap "Allow" on Apple's tracking prompt.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your data, a correction or a deletion, and more (see "Your rights"). We answer within one month.
  • You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete account.

Usage data (PostHog)

The app sends us information about how it is used, so we can find puzzles that are too hard or too easy, fix problems and decide what to build next. We use it only to improve the game. We do not use it for ads, and we do not sell it or share it with advertisers.

WhatExamples

We do not send your name, email, account ID, advertising ID, contacts, photos, exact location, or anything you type. PostHog does not keep a profile of you, does not keep your internet (IP) address with the data, and does not work out your location from it.

Who handles it: PostHog Inc., a company in the United States that stores and analyzes this data for us. Your usage data is stored on PostHog's US cloud servers in the United States. PostHog's privacy page: https://posthog.com/privacy.

How long PostHog keeps it: one year, then PostHog deletes it (PostHog data retention). We do not keep a longer copy.

Turning it off: everyone can switch usage data off at any time in Settings → Your data. Turning it off stops sending, drops any events still waiting, and clears your usage ID; turning it back on starts a fresh ID. Outside the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland, the switch is on by default. In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland, the app sends usage data, and saves its random ID on your phone, only if you say yes on the consent form (the same one used for ads), including up to about the last 30 to 40 seconds of play before you answer. If you say no or don't answer, nothing is sent. The switch there can only turn sending off; it never turns it on without your yes. You can change your consent answer at any time in Settings → Ads → Privacy choices.

Deleting your usage data

Because this data is tied only to the random ID on your phone, not to your account, deleting your account does not delete it from PostHog. Instead: Settings → Your data shows your usage ID, with a Copy ID button. Email that ID to support@bibleenigma.app. We will ask PostHog to delete that data and confirm to you when it is done, usually within a few weeks. Deleting your account or erasing the data on your device also removes the ID from your phone, so later usage data is not linked to the old one. Switching usage data off also clears the ID. If no ID is saved, Settings says "No usage data is sent from this phone." Data you have not asked us to delete is removed when PostHog's one-year retention period ends.

What stays on your device

Your puzzles in progress, settings and daily reminders stay on your phone. Reminders are scheduled on the device; no server is involved. The usage data described above is the only analytics the app sends.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your data;
  • correct it;
  • delete it;
  • limit how we use it, or object to a use (for usage data, you can also use the switch; see below the list);
  • give it to you in a form you can take elsewhere;
  • stop using it where you gave your consent (you can also withdraw consent in Settings → Ads → Privacy choices).

For usage data, you can object or ask us to limit its use by switching it off in Settings → Your data, or by emailing us. Switching it off stops new usage data. If you also want the data already sent deleted, email us your usage ID.

To ask by email, write to support@bibleenigma.app. We answer within one month. If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to the data protection authority where you live. For usage data we need your usage ID, which Settings → Your data shows (see "Deleting your usage data").

Deleting your data

  • Delete your account: open Settings → Delete account in the app, whether you are a guest or signed in. This permanently deletes your account, every sign-in linked to it (Apple, Google or email) and all your progress on our servers, all at once. It cannot be undone.
  • Erase this device: Settings → Erase data on this device clears progress stored on your phone. This is separate from deleting your account.
  • Stop Sign in with Apple or Google: you can also remove Bible Enigma from your Apple ID or Google account settings.
  • Usage data: see "Deleting your usage data" above.
  • Ads: you can reset or limit your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

We keep your account data until you delete your account. Usage data is kept as described in "Usage data" above. You can also email us (below) to use any of your rights, as described in "Your rights".

Children

Bible Enigma is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal data, email us and we will delete it.
